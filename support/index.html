Common questions

The hint says “Move left a little.” Who moves?

You do — the person holding the phone. In selfie mode the hint says “Move the phone left a little” instead.

Does it take the photo for me?

No. It only gives hints; it never presses the shutter. You can shoot at any time — the hints are just suggestions.

The cheat-sheet line doesn’t fit the moment.

Tap the card for another line. Press and hold to skip that kind of line for now.

I don’t see the camera view.

Open your iPhone’s Settings, allow Camera for Boyfriend Cam, and come back.

My photos aren’t being saved.

In your iPhone’s Settings, check that Boyfriend Cam is allowed to add photos.

Does it need the internet?

No. The camera view is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded.

What language is the app in?

For now, the app’s interface is in Traditional Chinese only.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 17 or later.
