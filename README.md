# Boyfriend Cam (男朋友相機) — a photo composition coach camera for iPhone

Boyfriend Cam is an iPhone camera that tells you where to move while you take her photo. A target ring and one short hint guide your framing, it gives you a line you can say to her, and when everything lines up it says "Now". After the shot you can try other compositions and aspect ratios and save a new copy. For iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/boyfriend-cam/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6834794357) · [Support](https://sona-orbit.github.io/boyfriend-cam/support/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/boyfriend-cam/privacy/)

## Features

- **One ring, one hint**: a target ring and a short hint such as "Move left a little". It never presses the shutter for you.
- **23 compositions**, showing at most 3 that fit the moment: thirds, centered, negative space, full body, half body, leading lines, frame in a frame, symmetry and more.
- **Fix what ruins a photo first**: a tilted phone, no room above her head, a cut at the knees or ankles, too little light.
- **Light tips**: backlight, a shadow across her face, harsh overhead sun.
- **Posing cheat sheet**: lines you can say to her in six kinds (eyes, expression, head and face, hands, body, movement); selfie mode has lines for yourself.
- **Shoot It Like This**: she frames the shot she wants, and her frame appears as an overlay for you to match.
- **Recompose after the shot**: crops for each fitting composition in Original, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1 or 9:16 (16:9 and 3:2 for horizontal photos), saved as a new photo.
- **Landscape and Object modes** with 18 guide lines, a Dining mode at the table, and 12 live filters. Runs on your iPhone and works offline.
