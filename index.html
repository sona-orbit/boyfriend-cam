Boyfriend CamiPhone

Taking her photo? Stop guessing the framing.

It opens straight to the camera and coaches you like a photographer: a ring and one short hint tell you where to move, and it gives you a line you can say to her. After the shot, try other compositions and ratios, and save a new copy.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later

What it does while you frame

  1. 01Reads the scene

    Where she’s standing, how busy the background is, whether there are lines or a frame — it looks while you frame.

  2. 02One short hint

    A target ring on screen and a few words, like “Move left a little.”

  3. 03Now

    When the dot lands in the ring, the hint turns to “Now.” Whether to shoot is up to you.

23 compositions — tap one

Each one shows the hint the app gives while you frame: not lined up yet, one small move, the dot slides into the ring — Now. A few only come up after the shot, when you recompose.

Light tips

Bad light? It tells you first

Backlight, a shadow across half her face, the sun straight overhead, a face too bright or too dark: one tip at a time. When she's backlit, her face is brightened a little for you, and the card after the shot notes the light.

For example
  • She's backlit — change direction
  • Move her into the shade
  • Too dark — move to brighter light
Shoot It Like This

She frames it, you match it

She frames the shot she wants and taps to save it. No photo is taken and it doesn't count toward your shots. She can also mark where she'll stand. Hand the phone over and her frame appears as an overlay. Line it up and you'll see Now.

For example
  • Frame it your way, then tap to save
  • Saved — hand it over
Dining

Sit down to eat, and the tips change

When she's eating or having coffee, a small Dining tag shows up in the viewfinder. The tips switch to ways of shooting at a table, and the cheat sheet switches to moves she can make in her seat. There's one more composition too: top-down on the table.

For example
  • Use 2× — her face looks smaller
  • Raise the phone to her eye level
  • Hold it flat, shoot from above

Recompose

After the shot, try another composition

It looks at the photo again and finds a crop for each composition that fits: thirds, half body, full body and more. Flip through them and pick a ratio — Original, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1 or 9:16. It only crops, never edits the picture, and saves a new photo. The original stays. For compositions that don’t fit, it tells you why.

Landscapes and objects

The camera gets two more modes, built around guide lines on the screen. There are 18 guides in all. There are 12 filters too.

Landscape mode

For a landscape, line it up first

Switch to Landscape and there is always a set of guide lines on screen. The app picks one for the scene in front of you, and you can change it. There are 10: Level, Grid, Horizon on a third, Halves, Leading lines, Diagonal, Symmetry, Foreground, Frame and Negative space. When it lines up, the lines change color and you see “Now”.

For things, lines for angle and placement

Filters

12 filters you see while framing

A Filters button sits to the left of the shutter. Pick one and the viewfinder shows that look right away, with a strength you can adjust; you can also change it on the card after the shot and while trying another composition. The 12 are Clear, Cream, Airy, Sunlit, Film, Cool, Retro, Cinema, Mono, Soft Mono, plus Vivid for landscapes and Tasty for food. The first slot is Original. Skin tones are kept as natural as possible. It is in the free version too, counted in your 3 shots a day.

Switching

One button, three modes

A mode button to the right of the shutter cycles through Portrait, Object and Landscape, and “Mode at launch” in Settings chooses which one opens first. The Composition guide gets Object and Landscape tabs too. Everything runs on your iPhone and works offline. It is in the free version too, counted in your 3 shots a day; Pro has no limit.

Questions

Does it take the photo for me?

No. It only gives hints; it never presses the shutter. You can shoot at any time — the hints are just suggestions.

Is it free?

It’s free to download, with 3 photos a day and every feature included. For unlimited photos, buy it once for NT$290 or subscribe for NT$190 a year (the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial). These are Taiwan prices; the App Store shows the price where you are.

Can I change the composition after the shot?

Yes. Recompose a photo as another composition that fits, in Original, 3:4, 4:5, 1:1 or 9:16. It only crops, saves a new photo, and leaves the original untouched.

Where are my photos saved?

In your iPhone’s photo library. There’s no account and no cloud storage of our own.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 17 or later.

Can I use it for selfies?

Yes. Switch to the front camera and the hints tell you how to move the phone; the cheat sheet switches to lines for yourself.

What language is the app in?

The app is in eleven languages: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil) and Vietnamese. It follows your iPhone’s language.
