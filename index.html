Boyfriend CamiPhone

Taking her photo?Stop guessing the framing.

It opens straight to the camera. As you frame the shot, Boyfriend Cam reads the scene and shows a target ring with one short hint. Line it up and it says “Now.” You can press the shutter any time.

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone · iOS 17 or later

What it does while you frame

  1. 01Reads the scene

    Where she’s standing, how busy the background is, whether there are lines or a frame — it looks while you frame.

  2. 02One short hint

    A target ring on screen and a few words, like “Move left a little.”

  3. 03Now

    When the dot lands in the ring, the hint turns to “Now.” Whether to shoot is up to you.

What it helps you with

Each feature comes with a situation and the hint the app would give. Hints are translated for this page — the app itself is currently in Traditional Chinese.

Framing hint

One ring, one line

While you frame, a target ring and a very short hint appear on screen. Move as it says; when the dot lands in the ring, you’ll see “Now.” It never takes the photo for you — the hints are only suggestions.

Cheat sheet

Not sure what to say to her? Just read this

Like a photographer would, it gives you a line you can say to her as is — small, doable moves in six kinds: eyes, expression, head and face, hands, body, and movement. In selfie mode, the lines are written for you.

Questions

Does it take the photo for me?

No. It only gives hints; it never presses the shutter. You can shoot at any time — the hints are just suggestions.

Does it need the internet?

No. The camera view is analyzed on your phone and is never uploaded.

Where are my photos saved?

In your iPhone’s photo library. There’s no account and no cloud storage of our own.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 17 or later.

Can I use it for selfies?

Yes. Switch to the front camera and the hints tell you how to move the phone; the cheat sheet switches to lines for yourself.

What language is the app in?

For now, the app’s interface is in Traditional Chinese only.

When will it be available?

It’s still being prepared, and there’s no date yet. Once it’s out, the button on this page will link straight to the App Store.
