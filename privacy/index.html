Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Boyfriend Cam is an iPhone camera app that gives you composition hints while you frame a shot. The camera view is analyzed only on your phone, there is no account, and the app does not collect your personal data. The only exception is a problem report you send yourself from the app (see below).

The camera view

While you frame a shot, the camera view is analyzed in real time on your iPhone to work out the composition and the hints. Neither the view nor the results are uploaded, sent to us, or sent to any third party.

Photos

Photos you take are saved to your iPhone’s photo library. The app only asks for permission to add photos, so it can save them there.

Account and personal data

There is no sign-up. The app does not collect your name, email address or any contact details, except contact details you choose to add when reporting a problem.

In-app purchases

Upgrades are App Store in-app purchases, and Apple handles the payment. The app does not collect or see your payment details.

Reporting a problem

Only when you tap Send in the app do we receive, in our Google spreadsheet, the problem you describe, any contact details you choose to add, the app version, system version, device model and interface language, and a random ID created at install that is used only to limit how many reports can be sent. We use them to look into the problem and reply to you. No photos, camera view, location, advertising identifier or data from the app are sent.

Ads and tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Children

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

No sale of data

We do not sell any of your data.

Apple

When you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles download, crash and similar data under its own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated.

This website

This website (sona-orbit.github.io) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives the pages you view, your browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This applies only to the website: the app does not include Google Analytics. You can block it in your browser’s privacy settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com
